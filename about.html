About Event Video Pros

A corporate event video team built by someone who has spent years in the back of ballrooms, where nothing gets a second take.

Who We Are

Event video, not general video

Event Video Pros is the corporate event division of R3 Media Production. We work almost entirely in conference centers, ballrooms, and hotel breakout rooms, which means we plan for the things that actually go wrong at events: agendas that slip, speakers who move, AV feeds that change an hour before doors.

Marketing teams and planners hire us because we hand back usable video quickly, and because they only have to talk to one person from the first planning call to the final file.

Who You'll Work With

Rob Spradling

Owner / Operator

Rob has spent 15 years in video production, including the last 6 producing live events: keynotes, conferences, and broadcasts where there are no second takes. That's the experience behind every project: calm under pressure, obsessive about details, and focused on making your event look as good on screen as it felt in the room.

Based in Colorado and traveling nationwide for multi-day conferences and touring programs. When you hire Event Video Pros, Rob is your point of contact from the first call to final delivery.
